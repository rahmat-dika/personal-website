Steel Plate Management System

Steel Plate Management System

Overview

Steel Plate Management System streamlines the planning and raw material preparation workflow for the production team, helping them manage steel plate layouts more efficiently before production begins.

The Challenge

The production team needed a system that could produce accurate planning data without a complicated, time-consuming input process. Early feedback made it clear that the biggest friction point wasn't the data itself, but the repetitive, form-heavy process users had to go through every time they needed to plan a new layout.

  • The need for a system capable of planning steel plate cutting.
  • Previously, the process involved a lot of paper and was still repetitive.
  • The need for a system capable of planning steel plate cutting
  • Streamline business processes to eliminate red tape and optimize the use of raw materials.

Case Information

  • Company : PT United Tractors Pandu Engineering
  • Date : Jan 2025 – Aug 2026
  • Progress : Go Live
  • User : Material Preparations Department
  • Location : UTPE

After streamlining business processes through an app


The Approach

As the frontend owner, I focused on making the interface as efficient as possible for the user's actual workflow. Based on user feedback that the form-filling process felt too complex, I redesigned the flow to store users' previous form entries in local storage — letting them simply edit small changes on a new entry instead of re-entering everything from scratch, while still preserving the accuracy of the data produced.

The Result

The simplified flow was well received by the production team, who no longer needed to repeat tedious manual entry for every new plan. The system struck a balance that mattered most to users: a faster, less complicated process without sacrificing data accuracy.
